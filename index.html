From discovery to decision

How to use a HooBuy spreadsheet

Build a useful shortlist

  1. Start with the product you need

    Choose a category and compare similar items. A descriptive title and clear pictures are more useful than a popularity badge.

  2. Open the source page

    Match the item ID, color and selected size. A catalog card is only a starting point; the live seller page controls available options.

  3. Check your purchasing route

    If you plan to use HooBuy, obtain the original marketplace link and confirm that your chosen service accepts that specific item. This directory does not convert links or confirm purchasing availability.

  4. Review the complete quote

    Compare item cost, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and applicable taxes before paying.

What does “spreadsheet” mean here?

People searching for a HooBuy spreadsheet are generally looking for an organized way to discover product links. This page presents a searchable web directory with a small imported catalog selection, rather than a downloadable Excel or Google Sheets file.

Keep the discovery page, seller listing and purchasing service separate in your notes. That makes it easier to return to the same item when a shared link changes or a color option disappears.

Keep an item record

Save the source URL, item ID, chosen variant, measurements and date checked. Record the final quoted price beside the catalog price so you can see what changed before checkout.

This is an independent information page. It is not operated by HooBuy, and HipobuyFind links do not constitute a HooBuy checkout.

Plan the delivered cost

Item price + local delivery + service or payment charges + international delivery + applicable duties and taxes = your comparison budget. Obtain a destination-specific quote; a low catalog price alone does not establish the cheapest delivered option.

Useful answers

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

A HooBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to research marketplace listings before purchasing through a shopping agent. This page provides a searchable web selection rather than a downloadable spreadsheet.

Where do the buttons take me?

The “HooBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons open HipobuyFind. They do not place an order or open a HooBuy checkout. Individual product cards open the corresponding HipobuyFind product pages.

Are the displayed prices the delivered cost?

No. Displayed CNY prices are catalog references. Delivery, service charges, currency conversion and applicable taxes may increase the total. Confirm the selected variant and final quote before paying.

Are these products verified for HooBuy?

No. The entries were read from HipobuyFind, but stock, seller quality, authenticity and individual HooBuy purchasing availability have not been verified. Confirm the original product link with your chosen service.

What should I do if a product link stops working?

Search the full HipobuyFind catalog using the descriptive product name or saved item ID. If you find a replacement, compare its photos, measurements and seller details rather than assuming it is identical.

Does this directory update automatically?

No. This page contains a static selection read on 19 November 2026. Search and sorting work within those four entries. The external catalog may show newer prices, different options or additional products.
